# Roadmap

The Runner is heading toward holding more than one app at a time, so the apps
you stage onto it can sit side by side on your phone.

## Next

- Named home-screen shortcuts: stage each app under its own name and pin it to
  the home screen with its own label and icon, from the released Runner. The
  plan is in [android-template's roadmap](https://github.com/PyDevices/android-template/blob/main/ROADMAP.md).
- Drop the launcher's own orientation wait when the Runner carries a
  pydevices release with pydevices#157, since the display then does that wait
  for every app. pydevices 0.7.0 doesn't have it yet.

Bugs, and things you need that don't work yet, go to
[issues](https://github.com/PyDevices/android-runner/issues).
